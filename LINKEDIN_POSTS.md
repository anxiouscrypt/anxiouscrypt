## Rule-Based Dev Assistant

I built a no-API developer assistant.

It uses deterministic rules and templates to turn commands like `create fastapi endpoint orders` into copy-ready snippets.

No LLM. No paid API. Just rules, templates, and command palette UX.

What I practiced:
- intent matching
- entity extraction
- template rendering
- assistant-style product thinking

Repo: https://github.com/anxiouscrypt/rule-based-dev-assistant
